About Atalaya

An atalaya is a watchtower: one lookout over academic posts and research funding across Spain.

What it does

Once a day, Atalaya reads official bulletins, funders and institutional job boards, extracts the open positions and calls, groups them by field and career stage, and links each one back to the original notice. It is non-commercial and has no accounts or tracking.

Accuracy

Always check the original notice before applying. Deadlines marked “est.” are calculated from wording such as “veinte días hábiles” and account for national and regional holidays, but not local ones. Where a notice lists its posts only in a PDF annex, an AI model reads the annex; those fields can contain mistakes.

For site owners

The crawler identifies itself as AtalayaBot, visits each page at most once a day, honours robots.txt and Crawl-delay, and only reads public pages. If you'd like your institution added, removed, or to provide a feed instead, please open an issue.
